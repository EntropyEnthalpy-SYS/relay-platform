# Complete target checklist — non-payment scope

This is the target specification, not a claim that every feature is implemented. It translates the 22 workstreams in `REQUIREMENTS.md` and applies the user's instruction to exclude payment development. Consult `RELEASE_0.115.0.md`, `NONPAYMENT_DELIVERY.md` and the verification evidence for actual completion.

The objective is a unified relay platform whose panel source, configuration, permissions, accounting and operations remain under the owner's control. Maintained protocol engines may be integrated with their source, version and license identified. Low-spec efficiency must be measured under realistic workloads.

## Definition of completion

Each applicable feature needs its data model, persistence, authorization, API, node execution, UI and meaningful acceptance evidence. Every unsupported combination needs an explicit rejection or an accurate compatibility notice. A source-available engine integration must identify upstream authorship and redistribution obligations; proprietary formats are not automatically reusable.

Functional equivalence and interoperability with another product are separate gates. In particular, original Hammer behavior, proprietary share-code formats and Snell compatibility remain conditional on usable specifications, permitted implementations and real client testing. They must not be represented as complete from a name or mock screen.

## Final delivery gates

- Two real Linux hosts and the agreed client/browser matrix, with exact hardware, OS, kernel, binaries, settings and network conditions recorded.
- Clean source rebuild, dependency/source/license inventory, package integrity checks and reproducibility results stated precisely.
- Cross-tenant/object isolation, credential revocation, failure injection and bounded resource behavior under hostile or malformed input.
- Clean backup restore, migration and recovery drills with traffic/accounting reconciliation.
- At least 72 hours of mixed traffic, restart, path failure and recovery, with no unexplained payload loss or accounting discrepancy.
- Comparable direct-baseline and competitor workloads under the same hardware and security settings before claiming top-tier performance.
- A hardware-sizing matrix covering native-only and optional-engine modes, rule/user count, concurrency, throughput, latency and memory peaks. Process caps must not be presented as whole-machine minimum RAM.

Payment remains excluded. A test domain/DNS provider, supported client decisions and additional hardware/network environments are inputs to the remaining applicable live acceptance gates.
